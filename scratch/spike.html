How to use
  1. Tab audio: pick a tab in the share dialog and tick “Also share tab audio.” Chrome only offers that checkbox for tab/screen shares, not windows.
  2. Test the real case — a vlog or Reel with speech over the music, not a clean track. That hit rate is the number that matters (PLAN.md Phase 0, Q3).
  3. Clips auto-download. Then: AUDD_TOKEN=… ./identify.sh clip-*.webm
  4. Record findings in docs/DECISIONS.md under the pending Phase 0 entry.
