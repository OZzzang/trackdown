How to use
  1. Start at 0% speech — that must match, or your music clip is bad and nothing below it means anything.
  2. Walk up the presets. The level where AudD flips to found:false is the answer to Q3.
  3. Each render downloads as mix-speech50-8s.webm — the ratio is in the filename, so results stay traceable.
  4. Identify each one: ./identify.sh ~/Downloads/mix-speech50-8s.webm | node parse.js
